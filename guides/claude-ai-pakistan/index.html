Why Pakistani Users Face Friction Subscribing to Claude Pro

While Claude 3.5 Sonnet is universally acclaimed by Pakistani developers, subscribing directly through Anthropic presents several hurdles:

  • Local Card Declines: Many Pakistani commercial banks (HBL, Meezan, UBL, Alfalah) decline transactions to overseas AI startups unless international e-commerce is explicitly enabled via banking apps.
  • Heavy Advance & Withholding Taxes: Section 236Y of the Pakistan Income Tax Ordinance imposes up to 10% withholding tax on non-filers (5% for filers) on international card spend, inflating a $20 subscription into a Rs. 6,500+ monthly expense.
  • No Local Wallets: Anthropic supports Stripe card checkout only, with zero integration for EasyPaisa, JazzCash, Raast, or SadaPay.
  • Strict Account Geoblocking: Using free VPNs to create Claude accounts frequently triggers automated fraud filters and prompt restrictions.
