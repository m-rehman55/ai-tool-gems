Canva Pro vs Canva Edu: What Pakistani Buyers Must Know

Many online sellers in Pakistan advertise lifetime Canva accounts for unrealistically cheap prices (e.g. Rs. 200). It is critical to understand the difference:

  1. Unverified Lifetime Accounts: Frequently get banned within 1 to 2 weeks by Canva when flagged, causing complete loss of saved designs.
  2. Verified Educational Workspaces: Clean, legitimate team invitations that preserve your personal account design history without sudden lockouts.
  3. Replacement Guarantee: Always ensure your purchase includes a 30-day replacement warranty so any disruption is resolved immediately.
