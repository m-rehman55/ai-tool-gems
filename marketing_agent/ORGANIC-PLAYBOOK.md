# AI Tool Gems — 3-Day Free Client Trial

Trial dates: **12–14 September 2026 (Pakistan time)**

This trial is designed to earn relevant visits without paid ads, fake engagement, unsolicited messages, or posting in communities that prohibit promotion. It cannot guarantee clients; it creates a measurable, repeatable acquisition path.

## What runs automatically

- Telegram channel: 3 product posts per day at 09:15, 13:30, and 18:45 PKT.
- Telegram bot: `/catalog`, `/deals`, `/contact`, and `/help` replies, checked every 30 minutes.
- Owner pack: one private Telegram delivery containing both morning and evening campaign details.
- Tracking: every promotional link carries a unique campaign code into the website and WhatsApp order message.
- Duplicate safety: a repository ledger claims a slot before a Telegram message can be published.
- Facebook, Instagram and TikTok: two audience-matched campaigns per day through Buffer, with platform/day-specific Pakistan times and tracked links.
- Each campaign: Gemini plus two different rotating products; morning and evening companion products do not repeat.
- Mixed-media delivery: every morning campaign is a readable 4:5 photo; every evening campaign is a photorealistic 10-second 9:16 video. Facebook and Instagram publish the video as a Reel; TikTok publishes it as video.
- Creative variety: colour palettes and companion products rotate automatically while prices and terms continue to come from the verified catalog.
- Learning: sent-post status and available engagement metrics are refreshed daily and stored against audience/media/time metadata.

## What the owner can review (no daily posting required)

1. Open the private Telegram content pack when convenient.
2. Check any Buffer or Telegram failure alert and reconnect only the affected channel if required.
3. Confirm current price, stock/access terms and warranty before taking payment.
4. Automated videos use the layered trend-inspired original soundbed. Do not replace it unless the exact track is cleared for commercial use in the destination platform's library. Native trending music requires notification/manual publishing because Buffer's automatic API cannot attach it.

## Posting-time evidence and tuning

Initial day-of-week slots use Buffer's 2026 analyses of [14 million Facebook posts](https://buffer.com/resources/best-time-to-post-on-facebook/), [9.6 million Instagram posts](https://buffer.com/resources/when-is-the-best-time-to-post-on-instagram/), and [7.1 million TikTok posts](https://buffer.com/resources/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok/). Those benchmarks are starting points, not promises. The agent records available delivery and engagement results so account-specific evidence can replace generic timing as enough observations accumulate.

Two Reels/videos per channel per day is a hard campaign limit. Captions disclose the promotion, avoid spam tags and fake urgency, and differ by slot. Duplicate locks prevent re-posting after a rerun. If a required video is missing or Buffer reports a platform error, that delivery stops and the owner receives an alert; it is not repeatedly retried into a possible spam signal.

## Community sharing rule

Only share where promotion is explicitly allowed. Read the group rules first, answer a real question, disclose that you sell the listing, and add a link only when it directly helps. Never paste the same message across groups, auto-DM members, buy followers, or invent reviews.

## Measure honestly

At the end of each day, note:

- channel members before/after;
- website visits visible in the hosting/analytics tool;
- WhatsApp inquiries containing a `Source code`;
- orders and revenue actually confirmed;
- questions or objections people repeatedly ask.

After day 3, keep the best product/angle combinations and rewrite weak ones. Do not call an angle a winner on one click or one message.

## Free growth loop after the trial

- Publish one useful comparison or buyer-answer each week on the website.
- Turn each answer into one Reel, one carousel, one Facebook post, and one Telegram post.
- Link social posts to the most relevant product or guide—not always the homepage.
- Ask real buyers for an honest review after delivery; never pre-write or incentivize a positive rating.
- Recheck displayed prices, access terms, and availability every week.
