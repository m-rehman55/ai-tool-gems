# AI Tool Gems — 3-Day Free Client Trial

Trial dates: **12–14 September 2026 (Pakistan time)**

This trial is designed to earn relevant visits without paid ads, fake engagement, unsolicited messages, or posting in communities that prohibit promotion. It cannot guarantee clients; it creates a measurable, repeatable acquisition path.

## What runs automatically

- Telegram channel: 3 product posts per day at 09:15, 13:30, and 18:45 PKT.
- Telegram bot: `/catalog`, `/deals`, `/contact`, and `/help` replies, checked every 30 minutes.
- Owner pack: one private Telegram delivery at 08:30 PKT with Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp Status, and Reel/TikTok copy.
- Tracking: every promotional link carries a unique campaign code into the website and WhatsApp order message.
- Duplicate safety: a repository ledger claims a slot before a Telegram message can be published.

## What the owner does each morning

1. Open the private content pack sent by the bot.
2. Post the Instagram caption to the AI Tool Gems account with a product visual.
3. Post the Facebook caption to the owned business page.
4. Add the short WhatsApp Status copy and tracked link.
5. Record the 15-second script as a screen recording or simple product-card video for Reels/TikTok.

No Instagram/Facebook credentials have been supplied, so the system does not claim to publish there automatically. Manual posting to owned accounts is free and keeps account control with the owner.

## Community sharing rule

Only share where promotion is explicitly allowed. Read the group rules first, answer a real question, disclose that you sell the listing, and add a link only when it directly helps. Never paste the same message across groups, auto-DM members, buy followers, or invent reviews.

## Measure honestly

At the end of each day, note:

- channel members before/after;
- website visits visible in the hosting/analytics tool;
- WhatsApp inquiries containing a `Source code`;
- orders and revenue actually confirmed;
- questions or objections people repeatedly ask.

After day 3, keep the best product/angle combinations and rewrite weak ones. Do not call an angle a winner on one click or one message.

## Free growth loop after the trial

- Publish one useful comparison or buyer-answer each week on the website.
- Turn each answer into one Reel, one carousel, one Facebook post, and one Telegram post.
- Link social posts to the most relevant product or guide—not always the homepage.
- Ask real buyers for an honest review after delivery; never pre-write or incentivize a positive rating.
- Recheck displayed prices, access terms, and availability every week.
