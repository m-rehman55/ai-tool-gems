Correction Policy — AIToolGems

Our Correction Policy

We correct errors promptly and transparently. Our correction process includes:

  • Error identification: We identify errors in product information, pricing, or availability.
  • Error correction: We correct errors within 24 hours of identification.
  • Correction notification: We notify affected users of corrections.
  • Correction log: We maintain a log of all corrections.

Reporting Errors

If you find an error, please contact us via WhatsApp. We will correct the error within 24 hours.

Correction Log

  • All corrections are logged with date, time, and description.
  • Corrections are verified before publishing.
  • Corrections are tracked for quality assurance.
