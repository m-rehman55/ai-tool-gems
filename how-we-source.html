How We Source — AIToolGems

Our Source Methodology

We verify all source information before publishing. Our source process includes:

  • Official source verification: We verify official sources against vendor websites.
  • Marketplace source verification: We verify marketplace sources against official sources.
  • Source link verification: We verify all source links before publishing.

Verification Process

  1. Collect source information from official vendor sources.
  2. Verify marketplace sources against official sources.
  3. Verify all source links before publishing.
  4. Publish verified sources with source links.
  5. Re-verify sources before each order.

Our Sourcing Process

We source AI tool subscriptions and digital products through a combination of official vendor programs, authorized resellers, and verified partner networks. Every source is vetted before we begin working with them, and we only partner with suppliers who can provide legitimate, working products.

Our sourcing team verifies each supplier's credentials, checks their track record, and conducts test purchases before adding any product to our marketplace.

Quality Checks

Before any product is listed, we run a comprehensive quality check. This includes verifying the product works as described, confirming the access type matches what we are selling, and ensuring the delivery method is reliable and fast.

We also check for any regional restrictions that might affect Pakistani users. If a product cannot be reliably delivered to Pakistan, we do not list it — no exceptions.

Supplier Relationships

We maintain long-term relationships with our suppliers based on consistent quality and reliable delivery. This allows us to offer competitive PKR pricing while maintaining the warranty and support standards our customers expect.

We regularly review our suppliers and replace any who fail to meet our quality benchmarks. Our customers' trust is more important than any single supplier relationship.
