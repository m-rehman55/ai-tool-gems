# SEO TRUST POLICY

## Trust Signals

### Present
- About page ✅
- Contact page ✅
- Privacy policy ✅
- Terms of service ✅
- Review methodology ✅
- How we review page ✅

### Missing
- Author/editor information ❌
- Verification dates ❌
- Pricing methodology ❌
- Source methodology ❌
- Correction policy ❌
- Editorial policy ❌

## E-E-A-T Requirements

1. Experience: First-hand marketplace information ✅
2. Expertise: Review methodology present ⚠️
3. Authoritativeness: Original data assets missing ❌
4. Trustworthiness: Trust signals partial ⚠️

## Trust Rules

- Never fabricate credentials
- Never fake reviews
- Never fake ratings
- Never fake authors
- Never fake expertise
- Clearly distinguish official provider info vs marketplace info
- State what is actually true
- Verify before publishing

## Marketplace Transparency

Explain:
- What users purchase
- Access method
- Account/shared account rules
- Duration
- Limitations
- Warranty
- Support
- Refund rules
- Delivery
- Privacy considerations
