PRIVACY AND ACCOUNT CONTROL

Privacy policy

This policy explains what information AI Tool Gems uses on its website and when an account owner enables an optional social publishing integration.

Information used by the website

The public marketplace may store cart preferences in the visitor's browser. Campaign source parameters may be carried into a WhatsApp order link so the customer can tell us where they found an offer. The website does not collect payment-card details, passwords, or one-time codes.

TikTok integration data

When an account owner chooses to connect TikTok, the integration may receive the authorized account identifier, basic profile information, access and refresh tokens, publishing permissions, content selected for publishing, and post-processing status. This information is used only to authenticate the connection, publish or upload content authorized by the account owner, prevent duplicate submissions, and report whether a requested post succeeded.

Storage, sharing, and retention

Authentication credentials are intended to be kept in protected server-side secret storage and are not placed in public website code. We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate the requested feature, such as TikTok for publishing, GitHub for automated workflows, Telegram for private owner reports, and WhatsApp when a visitor chooses to contact us. Authorization information is retained only while the integration remains connected or as needed for security and operational records.

Your choices and deletion

You may revoke the app from TikTok's connected-app permissions. You may also request disconnection and deletion of stored integration data through AI Tool Gems WhatsApp support. We will verify that the request comes from the account owner before removing protected account data.

Security and children

Do not send passwords, one-time codes, or full payment-card details. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. The integration is intended for an adult business account owner and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be published on this page with a revised date. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to +92 323 6715731 on WhatsApp.

Data We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to process your order and deliver your product. This includes your name, WhatsApp number, order details (product name, access type, duration), and payment method confirmation. We do not collect or store your full payment card details.

When you browse our website, we may collect standard analytics data such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. This data is used solely to improve our website and services. We do not sell or share this data with third parties for marketing purposes.

How We Use Your Information

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders
  • Communicating with you about your order status and delivery
  • Providing customer support and handling warranty claims
  • Improving our website, products, and services
  • Complying with legal obligations and preventing fraud

We do not use your personal information for any purpose beyond those described in this policy without your explicit consent.

Data Security

We take data security seriously. Your personal information is stored on secure servers with access restricted to authorized personnel only. We use industry-standard encryption for data transmission and storage.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, including any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. Order records are typically retained for 2 years for warranty and accounting purposes, after which they are securely deleted.

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us on WhatsApp. We will process your request within 30 days, except where we are legally required to retain certain information.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies to improve your browsing experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us remember your preferences and understand how you use our site.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but please note that some features of our website may not function properly without them.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to process payments (such as EasyPaisa, JazzCash, and bank transfers) and to send WhatsApp messages. These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

We do not share your personal information with third parties except as necessary to fulfill your order or as required by law.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time. You also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing of your data. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us on WhatsApp.

We will respond to your request within 30 days and take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
