How We Review — AIToolGems

Our Review Methodology

We verify all product information before publishing. Our review process includes:

  • Price verification: We check pricing against official vendor sources and marketplace listings.
  • Feature verification: We verify product features against official documentation.
  • Availability verification: We confirm availability via WhatsApp before publishing.
  • Delivery verification: We verify delivery times and methods.
  • Warranty verification: We confirm warranty and replacement policies.

Verification Process

  1. Collect product information from official vendor sources.
  2. Verify pricing against marketplace listings.
  3. Confirm availability via WhatsApp.
  4. Publish verified information with source links.
  5. Re-verify pricing and availability before each order.

Sources

  • Official vendor websites
  • Marketplace listings
  • WhatsApp verification
