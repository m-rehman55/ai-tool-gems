How We Review — AIToolGems

Our Review Methodology

We verify all product information before publishing. Our review process includes:

  • Price verification: We check pricing against official vendor sources and marketplace listings.
  • Feature verification: We verify product features against official documentation.
  • Availability verification: We confirm availability via WhatsApp before publishing.
  • Delivery verification: We verify delivery times and methods.
  • Warranty verification: We confirm warranty and replacement policies.

Verification Process

  1. Collect product information from official vendor sources.
  2. Verify pricing against marketplace listings.
  3. Confirm availability via WhatsApp.
  4. Publish verified information with source links.
  5. Re-verify pricing and availability before each order.

Sources

  • Official vendor websites
  • Marketplace listings
  • WhatsApp verification

Our Review Methodology

Every product listed on AI Tool Gems goes through a multi-step review process before it appears on our marketplace. We test, verify, and document each listing to ensure our customers get exactly what they pay for.

Our review process checks five key areas: pricing accuracy, feature availability, access type verification, delivery method, and warranty terms. Only products that pass all five checks are listed.

What We Verify

  • Pricing — We confirm the official vendor price and calculate our PKR marketplace price with full transparency
  • Features — We verify that the features advertised are actually included in the plan being sold
  • Access Type — We confirm whether the product is Private, Shared, Invitation-based, or a License Key
  • Delivery — We test the delivery method and confirm the estimated delivery time
  • Warranty — We document the exact replacement warranty period for each product

Ongoing Monitoring

Our review does not end at listing. We monitor vendor changes, price updates, and feature availability continuously. If a product no longer meets our quality standards, we remove it from the marketplace immediately and notify affected customers.

We also track customer feedback and delivery success rates. Products with consistently poor performance are removed, regardless of their popularity.
