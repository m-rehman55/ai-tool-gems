OUR LISTING METHOD

How we review AI tool listings

AI Tool Gems reviews every marketplace listing for identity, feature accuracy, price clarity, access conditions, delivery expectations, and support terms. This process helps customers compare offers in Pakistani rupees without confusing a marketplace listing with an official vendor sale.

What does AI Tool Gems verify?

We first match each product name and logo to the relevant vendor and consult the vendor’s public product or pricing information for the advertised capabilities. We then review the marketplace offer separately: the PKR price, duration, access type, estimated delivery window, and replacement-warranty period must all be stated on the listing. Before payment, WhatsApp support reconfirms current availability and the exact access conditions. If a vendor changes its product features, limits, name, or availability, the listing is updated when the change is identified. Marketplace prices are not presented as the vendor’s official retail prices. AI Tool Gems remains an independent marketplace and does not claim endorsement, partnership, or authorization from a third-party brand unless that relationship is explicitly documented on the relevant page.

Primary sources used for feature checks

  • OpenAI — ChatGPT plans and features
  • Google One — Google AI plans
  • Canva — plan comparison
  • Adobe — Creative Cloud plans
  • NordVPN — plans
  • Surfshark — plans

How access types are labeled

Private indicates individual access under the terms confirmed before payment. Shared indicates access subject to shared-account conditions. Invitation means the customer is added to a workspace, team, family, or plan. License Key means a digital activation key is delivered. Customers should review device limits, renewal, ownership, and password-control details with support before ordering.

Corrections and update policy

Listings are reviewed when prices, availability, or plan conditions change and before an order is accepted. If you identify an outdated feature or unclear offer, send the product URL and correction through the contact page. The catalog team checks the report against an official source and updates the listing where needed.

Method and catalog reviewed: 11 September 2026

Buying information remains separate

Vendor sources are used to check product identity and public features. The marketplace’s PKR price, delivery estimate, access type, warranty, and refund process are governed by the offer confirmed on WhatsApp and the AI Tool Gems policies.
