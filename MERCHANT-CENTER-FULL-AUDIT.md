## 1. Executive Summary

This comprehensive, evidence-based audit was conducted to investigate and resolve all active diagnostic disapprovals and policy violations affecting the **AI Tool Gems Pakistan** Google Merchant Center account (`5877049045`).

### Current Verified Merchant Center Diagnostics
1. **Misrepresentation (Account-Level & Product-Level):** 38/38 products disapproved.
2. **Missing Korean Business Registration Number:** 20 products flagged.
3. **Missing Shipping Information in Some Countries:** 20 products flagged (South Korea / international).
4. **Unsupported Currency:** South Korea listings flagged for submitting PKR in a KR-targeted listing without active currency conversion.
5. **Image Too Small:** Flagged on at least one product (repository audit identified 9 products with images $< 100 \times 100$ px).
6. **One Critical Account-Level Policy Issue:** Account-level suspension warning/disapproval for Misrepresentation.

### Root Cause Summary
- **Diagnostic #1 & #6 (Misrepresentation):** Google’s automated merchant review and enforcement crawlers flagged the domain for:
  - Absence of a physical operating street address and direct contact email on the public `contact.html` page (only a WhatsApp link was initially presented).
  - Transition from website to a conversational WhatsApp chat for order finalization rather than a traditional automated web shopping cart and checkout.
  - Lack of documented OEM authorization certificates for reselling third-party software subscriptions (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, Canva, Adobe, etc.).
  - Listing digital software subscriptions with monthly durations ($< 12$ months) and shared multi-user accounts, both of which conflict directly with Google Merchant Center's *Software Subscriptions* and *Enabling Dishonest Behavior* policies.
- **Diagnostics #2, #3 & #4 (South Korea Issues):** These three errors stem from an **account-level targeting misconfiguration** in Merchant Center. South Korea (`KR`) was inadvertently included in the target countries or feed destination settings. Under South Korean e-commerce regulations, all commercial merchants must submit a government-issued Business Registration Number (BRN). Furthermore, the feed contained shipping rules only for Pakistan (`PK`) and prices only in Pakistani Rupees (`PKR`).
- **Diagnostic #5 (Image Too Small):** Google Merchant Center requires all non-apparel product images to be at least $100 \times 100$ pixels. Nine product logos in `assets/product-logos/` were $48 \times 48$ or $64 \times 64$ pixels.

## 3. Systematic Investigation of Observed Diagnostics

### Diagnostic 1: Misrepresentation (38/38 Products Affected)
- **Policy Reference:** [Google Merchant Center Misrepresentation Policy](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6150127)
- **Observed Finding:**
  1. *Contact Information:* `contact.html` previously provided only a WhatsApp phone number (`+92 323 6715731`). It lacked a visible official support email (`support@aitoolgems.tech`), a documented physical operating location, and formal business hours.
  2. *Commercial Purchase Model:* When Google Shopping crawlers evaluate product landing pages, they expect an immediate "Add to Cart" or direct online checkout with payment gateway processing. AI Tool Gems employs an assisted model where users click "Order on WhatsApp" to coordinate domestic payment via EasyPaisa, JazzCash, SadaPay, or Bank IBFT. Automated evaluators classify conversational checkouts without upfront merchant credentials as "Misrepresentation / Incomplete Checkout".
  3. *Independent Reseller Identity:* While `policies.html`, `about.html`, and `terms-of-service` prominently declare that AI Tool Gems is an independent facilitator not affiliated with OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, or Canva, Google’s automated review requires documentary authorization before allowing third-party software trademarks on Google Shopping.
  4. *Software Term Requirements:* Google Shopping explicitly prohibits software subscriptions with durations under 12 months (see Section 4).
- **Severity:** `CRITICAL` (Account-Blocking)

### Diagnostic 2: Missing Korean Business Registration Number (20 Products)
- **Policy Reference:** [South Korea Business Registration Requirements for Google Shopping](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/9011747)
- **Observed Finding:**
  South Korea's *Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce* mandates that all merchants displaying products to consumers in South Korea register with the Fair Trade Commission and provide their Business Registration Number (사업자등록번호) in their Merchant Center business information.
  AI Tool Gems is a Pakistani domestic service. It does not possess a Korean corporate entity or registration. South Korea was mistakenly included in target countries.
- **Severity:** `HIGH` (Resolved via Country Targeting Configuration)

### Diagnostic 3: Missing Shipping Information in Some Countries (20 Products)
- **Policy Reference:** [Google Merchant Center Shipping Settings](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6069284)
- **Observed Finding:**
  The submitted product feed specified `<g:shipping><g:country>PK</g:country><g:price>0.00 PKR</g:price></g:shipping>`. Because South Korea was enabled as a target country in Merchant Center, Google evaluated shipping coverage for KR. Finding no shipping rules for South Korea, Google disapproved all 20 products for missing shipping information.
- **Severity:** `HIGH` (Resolved via Country Targeting Configuration)

### Diagnostic 4: Unsupported Currency — South Korea Listings
- **Policy Reference:** [Currency Requirements for Google Shopping](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/160637)
- **Observed Finding:**
  The feed provided prices in Pakistani Rupees (`2300.00 PKR`). For South Korean listings, Google requires South Korean Won (`KRW`) or an active automated Currency Conversion configuration. Submitting PKR prices into a South Korea target profile without currency conversion triggered immediate disapproval.
- **Severity:** `HIGH` (Resolved via Country Targeting Configuration)

### Diagnostic 5: Image Too Small (At Least One Product)
- **Policy Reference:** [Google Merchant Center Image Specifications](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6324350)
- **Observed Finding:**
  Google Shopping requires a minimum image dimension of $100 \times 100$ pixels for non-apparel items. A complete programmatic inspection of `assets/product-logos/` revealed **9 product logos below $100 \times 100$ pixels**:
  - `capcut.png` ($64 \times 64$)
  - `chatprd.png` ($48 \times 48$)
  - `firecrawl.png` ($48 \times 48$)
  - `grammarly.png` ($48 \times 48$)
  - `hailuo-ai.png` ($64 \times 64$)
  - `heygen.png` ($48 \times 48$)
  - `linkedin.png` ($64 \times 64$)
  - `n8n.png` ($48 \times 48$)
  - `netflix.png` ($64 \times 64$)
- **Severity:** `MEDIUM` (Fixed programmatically on working branch)

### Diagnostic 6: Critical Account-Level Policy Issue
- **Policy Reference:** [Merchant Center Account Disapprovals & Warnings](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/2948694)
- **Observed Finding:**
  When Google disapproves 100% of submitted offers under Misrepresentation, it triggers an account-level suspension notice. This prevents any free listings or Shopping ads from serving worldwide until the root causes are remediated and verified.
- **Severity:** `CRITICAL` (Requires Owner Approval & Manual Review Request)

## 4. Digital Software Subscriptions Policy Analysis

A critical finding of this audit is that **the majority of AI software subscription products are inherently ineligible for Google Merchant Center (Google Shopping / Free Product Listings)** under Google’s current software policy:

> **Google Merchant Center Computer Software Subscriptions Rule:**  
> *"You can only submit subscriptions for computer software (excluding video games) if:  
> 1. The software is sold with a prepaid subscription for a duration of at least 1 year (12 months); OR  
> 2. It is purchased on a recurring basis, but only if prepaid annually.  
> Subscriptions with billing periods of less than one year (such as monthly subscriptions) are NOT ELIGIBLE for Google Shopping feeds."*

### Application to AI Tool Gems Catalog
- **26 out of 40 assisted tools** are sold as 1-month, 2-month, or 3-month access passes (e.g., ChatGPT Plus 1-Month, Claude Pro 1-Month, Midjourney 1-Month). These violate the 12-month prepaid rule and **cannot compliantly be submitted to Google Shopping**.
- **7 tools** utilize shared account credentials (`netflix`, `semrush`, `surfshark`, `veo`, `quillbot-shared`, etc.). Selling shared access violates Google's *Enabling Dishonest Behavior* and *Misrepresentation* policies.
- **1 tool** (`turnitin`) provides student accounts for plagiarism check bypass, which strictly violates Google’s *Academic Dishonesty Policy*.
- **2 tools** (`windows`, `office365`) are Microsoft software keys, requiring verified Microsoft Authorized Partner documentation.
- **2 tools** (`canva`, `gemini`) use team workspace invitations, requiring direct OEM distribution rights.
- **25 directory profiles** (`ahrefs`, `v0`, `make`, etc.) are non-transactional knowledge bases and must never be included in commercial feeds.

**Conclusion:** The automated feed generator (`merchant_agent/feed_generator.py`) was correct to implement an offer-level hold policy (`eligible_only=True`). Ineligible digital goods should never be force-submitted to Google Shopping, as doing so guarantees immediate domain suspension.
