## 1. Google Merchant Center Software Subscription Requirements
Google Merchant Center policy for software subscriptions requires:
1. **Prepaid Term:** The customer must be able to purchase a prepaid term of at least 12 months.
2. **Price Disclosure:** Full subscription cost for the entire duration must be prominently displayed.
3. **No Automatic Recurring Hidden Billing:** Renewal conditions must be transparent.
4. **Resale Rights:** The merchant must have verified authorization or legitimate resale rights.

### Merchant Gate Eligibility Matrix:
- **Eligible for Shopping Feed:** Only verified prepaid 12-Month+ software licenses with authentic consumer rights (e.g., Office 365 Home/Family prepaid keys, Windows Pro retail keys).
- **Ineligible / HOLD for Shopping Feed:**
  * 1-Day, 7-Day, or 14-Day temporary accounts.
  * Shared invitation team links without verified transfer authorization.
  * Platform token API credits (Codex API tokens, Claude credits).
  * Recurring monthly subscriptions billed manually via chat.
