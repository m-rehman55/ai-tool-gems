## 1. Regulatory Separation of Concerns: Google Merchant Center vs. Google Ads

A fundamental principle of digital product compliance is that Google Merchant Center and Google Ads operate under distinct policies, technical specifications, and legal enforcement mechanisms:

| Policy Dimension | Google Merchant Center (Shopping & Free Product Listings) | Google Ads (Search Text Ads) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Governing Policy** | **Software Subscriptions Policy** & Product Feed Specifications | **Third-Party Reseller Policy** & Misrepresentation Guidelines |
| **Duration Requirement** | Software subscriptions require a prepaid annual term of **at least 12 months** or perpetual retail license keys. Short-term (1-month, 2-month, 3-month) subscriptions are **strictly ineligible**. | Terms of any duration (1 month, 3 months, 1 year) are permitted, provided pricing and terms are clearly disclosed in the ad and landing page. |
| **Access Model** | Shared accounts, team invitation slots, and assisted third-party logins are **strictly prohibited** under Misrepresentation and Dishonest Behavior policies. | Private accounts with flat upfront PKR pricing and replacement warranties are **eligible**, provided clear third-party non-affiliation disclosures are present. |
| **Current Catalog Status** | **0 / 40 Active Offers Eligible** (100% safely placed on policy **HOLD** to protect Merchant Center account integrity). | **31 / 40 Active Offers Eligible** with mandatory non-affiliation disclaimers (6 Shared plans, 2 OEM keys, and 1 academic on HOLD). |

## 2. Inviolable Commercial Guardrails

1. **Never Hide a Recurring Price:** Every offer is sold as a flat, prepaid transaction in Pakistani Rupees (`PKR`). There is zero automatic card debiting, zero stealth recurring renewals, and zero hidden charges.
2. **Never Misrepresent an Official Affiliation:** AIToolGems explicitly identifies as an independent digital subscription provider. No claim of being OpenAI, Anthropic, Midjourney, Google, or Microsoft is permitted.
3. **Never Advertise an Unavailable Offer:** Real-time stock status is synchronized with inventory; out-of-stock items are suppressed immediately.
4. **Never Use Bait-and-Switch Pricing:** Landing page price, cart price, structured data schema price, and ad headline price match 100% in PKR.
5. **Never Misrepresent Access Ownership:** Each tool explicitly states whether the customer receives a **Private Dedicated Account**, a **Direct Workspace Invitation**, or a **Private Portal Login**. Shared accounts are clearly labeled as shared and excluded from public ad feeds.
6. **Never Create Misleading Merchant Feeds:** Items failing Google Merchant Center Shopping requirements are never forced into feeds. They are cleanly segregated into `merchant_hold_register.json`.
7. **Strict Supplier Privacy:** Zero private wholesale vendor names, bot handles, or wholesale prices are ever exposed in public documentation or code.

## 5. Mandatory Project Distinctions (Standard Operating Model)

In accordance with project governance rules, this Offer Eligibility & Risk Review explicitly distinguishes:

### 1. Verified Facts
- Google Merchant Center and Google Ads operate under completely separate policy frameworks.
- Zero of the 40 active storefront tools currently meet Google Merchant Center Shopping software subscription criteria (prepaid 12-month+ term with OEM resale certificate). All 40 have been safely placed on policy **HOLD** in `merchant_hold_register.json`.
- 31 of 40 active tools are eligible for Google Ads Search Text Ads with mandatory third-party non-affiliation disclosures.
- All 18 cautionary entries (CAT-158 to CAT-175) are 100% blocked from the public storefront, search indexing, and ad feeds.
- Zero wholesale supplier names, bot handles, or wholesale prices are included in this report or exposed in public code.

### 2. Historical Baseline Data
- Earlier catalog drafts lumped digital access offers into generic categories without evaluating Google's 12-month software subscription rule.
- Initial wholesale lists contained high-risk entries (IDs 158–175) that lacked automated isolation controls.

### 3. New Observations
- Proactively segregating ineligible items into `merchant_hold_register.json` completely protects Google Merchant Center account standing from algorithmic suspension.
- Clear non-affiliation disclaimers ("Independent Provider — Not Affiliated with OpenAI") on landing pages satisfy Google Ads Third-Party Reseller guidelines while building consumer trust in Pakistan.

### 4. Hypotheses
- Applying for direct enterprise reseller authorization for 1-year plans (e.g. Gamma, Perplexity, Figma, Notion) will eventually enable compliant Google Shopping feeds.
- Focusing advertising spend exclusively on Google Ads Search Text Ads for high-intent queries (e.g. "buy chatgpt in pakistan") will yield significantly higher ROAS than Google Shopping.

### 5. Unavailable Data
- Official Google Merchant Center API verification responses for individual software feed items are unavailable because no live Merchant Center OAuth credentials have been provisioned in `.env`.
- Real-world Google Ads conversion costs and impression share are unavailable because no active ad spend has been deployed.

### 6. Work Completed
- Completed offer-level policy assessment across all 40 storefront products and 18 high-risk entries.
- Created `merchant_hold_register.json` isolating all 40 products from Merchant feeds to protect account health.
- Enforced hard architectural blocks on high-risk categories (OTP, webmail resale, financial accounts).
- Prepared compliant search ad copy guidelines with mandatory non-affiliation disclaimers.

### 7. Work Blocked
- Direct submission of product feeds to Google Merchant Center is blocked until:
  1. The owner secures direct OEM enterprise reseller agreements.
  2. Products offer prepaid 12-month+ billing terms.

### 8. Owner Decisions Required
- Owner must decide whether to pursue direct OEM reseller partnerships for annual software subscriptions.
- Owner approval required before launching any Google Ads Search campaigns or allocating advertising spend.
