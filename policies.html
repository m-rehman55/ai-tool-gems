Order confirmation & anti-fraud safety rules

  • No automated charges: You will never be billed automatically. Every transaction requires your explicit manual transfer and mutual confirmation.
  • Receipt verification: After transferring funds via EasyPaisa, JazzCash, SadaPay, or Bank IBFT, send the payment receipt/transaction ID to our official WhatsApp support (+92 323 6715731). Support verifies the funds in the merchant ledger before delivering login credentials or digital license keys.
  • Security warning: AI Tool Gems support will never ask for your ATM PIN, OTP code, bank password, or credit card CVV. Do not share sensitive banking credentials with anyone.
  • Official contact protection: Only transfer funds to account details confirmed via our verified business WhatsApp channel. Account credentials and payment IDs are never published statically to prevent impersonation fraud.

Delivery starts after payment is confirmed. The estimate on each listing is not a guaranteed deadline because product availability and third-party activation systems can change. Support will notify the customer if an order is delayed or unavailable.

A refund is not automatically available for change of mind, misuse, or incompatibility that was disclosed before payment. Any exception is confirmed in writing on WhatsApp.
