Order and support policies

These policies explain the marketplace process. The exact product duration, access type, delivery estimate, and replacement period shown on the selected listing are reconfirmed on WhatsApp before payment.

Access types

Private means the listing is intended for individual access. Shared means access may be used under shared-account conditions. Invitation means access is provided by an invitation to a workspace, team, or plan. License Key means a digital activation key is delivered. Ask support about password control, device limits, renewal, and account ownership before paying.

Digital delivery

Delivery starts after payment is confirmed. The estimate on each listing is not a guaranteed deadline because product availability and third-party activation systems can change. Support will notify the customer if an order is delayed or unavailable.

Replacement warranty

Replacement coverage applies for the period displayed on the product page and begins when access is delivered. Customers should contact support promptly with the product name and a description of the access issue. Coverage is assessed against the plan details confirmed before payment. It does not cover loss caused by sharing credentials, prohibited account changes, misuse, or violating third-party service rules.

Refund policy

If AI Tool Gems confirms an order but cannot deliver the agreed product, the customer may request a refund. After working access or a digital key has been delivered, support first evaluates replacement eligibility under the displayed warranty. A refund is not automatically available for change of mind, misuse, or incompatibility that was disclosed before payment. Any exception is confirmed in writing on WhatsApp.

Privacy and safe communication

WhatsApp is used for product questions and order coordination. Customers should never send passwords, one-time codes, full card details, or unrelated identity documents. The website uses browser storage for cart preferences; it does not currently publish a customer database or accept payment-card details directly.

Third-party brands

AI Tool Gems is an independent marketplace and is not affiliated with or endorsed by listed third-party brands unless explicitly stated. Names and logos identify products for comparison. Plan features and vendor terms may change; customers should confirm the current offer before payment.
