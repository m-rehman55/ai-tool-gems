How We Price — AIToolGems

Our Pricing Methodology

We verify all pricing information before publishing. Our pricing process includes:

  • Official price check: We check pricing against official vendor websites.
  • Marketplace price check: We verify marketplace pricing against official sources.
  • PKR equivalent: We calculate PKR equivalents using current exchange rates.
  • Price change tracking: We track price changes from previous verified prices.

Verification Process

  1. Collect pricing information from official vendor sources.
  2. Verify marketplace pricing against official sources.
  3. Calculate PKR equivalents using current exchange rates.
  4. Publish verified pricing with source links.
  5. Re-verify pricing before each order.

Sources

  • Official vendor websites
  • Marketplace listings
  • WhatsApp verification

Last updated: 2026-10-01

How We Determine PKR Prices

Our pricing process is straightforward and transparent. We track the official vendor price for every product, apply our marketplace margin, and display the final PKR price clearly on each product page. We do not use hidden fees or surprise charges.

Prices are reviewed and updated regularly to reflect changes in official vendor pricing or exchange rates. When a price changes, we update the listing immediately and note the last verified date so you know the information is current.

Price Match Guarantee

If you find a lower verified price for the same product with the same access type and warranty terms from another Pakistani seller, contact us on WhatsApp. We will review the offer and do our best to match or beat it — because fair pricing matters to us.

Transparent Savings

Every product card shows you three things: the official reference price, our marketplace price, and the percentage you save. This way, you always know exactly what you are paying for and how much you are saving compared to buying directly from the vendor.
