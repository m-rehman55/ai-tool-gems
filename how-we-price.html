How We Price — AIToolGems

Our Pricing Methodology

We verify all pricing information before publishing. Our pricing process includes:

  • Official price check: We check pricing against official vendor websites.
  • Marketplace price check: We verify marketplace pricing against official sources.
  • PKR equivalent: We calculate PKR equivalents using current exchange rates.
  • Price change tracking: We track price changes from previous verified prices.

Verification Process

  1. Collect pricing information from official vendor sources.
  2. Verify marketplace pricing against official sources.
  3. Calculate PKR equivalents using current exchange rates.
  4. Publish verified pricing with source links.
  5. Re-verify pricing before each order.

Sources

  • Official vendor websites
  • Marketplace listings
  • WhatsApp verification
