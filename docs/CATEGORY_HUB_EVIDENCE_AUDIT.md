## 4. Search Engine Rich Snippet Eligibility Disclosure

### Technical Analysis:
Under Google's August 2023 Structured Data update, `FAQPage` rich snippets in Google Search Results Pages (SERPs) are restricted primarily to authoritative government and health domains. For commercial and e-commerce websites, `FAQPage` structured data remains valid Schema.org syntax and is parsed by Google, Bing, and AI Answer Engines (Perplexity, Gemini, ChatGPT) for entity grounding and factual retrieval, but Google SERP rich snippets are **not guaranteed**.

### Hub Disclosure Text Implemented:
```html
<p class="faq-disclaimer" style="font-size: 0.85rem; color: var(--text-muted, #94a3b8); margin-top: 1.5rem; line-height: 1.4;">

Under Google Search Central guidelines, rich snippet display in search engine results pages is determined algorithmically by Google and is not guaranteed.</em>
</p>
```
