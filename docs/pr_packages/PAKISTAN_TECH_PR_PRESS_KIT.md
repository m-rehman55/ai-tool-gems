## 2. Official Press Release (Full Copy)

### FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

### AI Tool Gems Launches Pakistan’s First Machine-Readable AI Software Pricing Index to Bridge the Affordability and Payment Gap for Tech Talent

**ISLAMABAD / LAHORE / KARACHI, PAKISTAN — November 2026** — As artificial intelligence rapidly reshapes the global digital economy, tech professionals, digital agencies, and students across Pakistan face an invisible barrier: foreign currency payment restrictions, unpredictable banking exchange surcharges, and a lack of transparent localized subscription data.

Today, **AI Tool Gems** (https://aitoolgems.tech/) announced the launch of the **Pakistan AI Software Pricing & Domestic Payment Rails Index**, an open-access data initiative documenting real-world subscription costs in Pakistani Rupees (PKR) and domestic payment options for 40 industry-standard AI productivity and coding platforms.

#### Bridging the Credit Card Divide in Emerging Markets
While platforms such as OpenAI, Anthropic, and Midjourney operate primarily on Western credit card rails, a significant segment of Pakistani tech workers relies on domestic fintech ecosystems such as EasyPaisa, JazzCash, SadaPay, NayaPay, and Raast instant bank transfers. 

The AI Tool Gems index tracks flat PKR pricing parity across categories including:
* **AI Coding & Development:** Cursor AI Pro, GitHub Copilot, Replit Core, Windsurf, Bolt.new.
* **Conversational AI & Research:** ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, Google Gemini Advanced, Perplexity Pro.
* **Generative Video & Media:** CapCut Pro, Runway Gen-3, Midjourney v6.1, Kling AI, HeyGen.
* **Academic & Writing Tools:** Grammarly Premium, QuillBot Premium, Turnitin, Gamma App.

#### Fighting Deceptive "Lifetime" Scams
A major challenge facing digital buyers in South Asia is the proliferation of deceptive resellers advertising "lifetime access" to subscription-based AI models—claims that frequently end in account bans and financial loss within weeks.

*"Our core philosophy is absolute commercial truth,"* said M. Rehman Farooq, founder of AI Tool Gems. *"AI models operate on heavy GPU compute and monthly vendor billing; 'lifetime' access simply does not exist. We provide clear, guaranteed subscription terms—from 1 Month to 1 Year—backed by verified replacement warranties and authentic domestic payment rails."*

#### Open Machine-Readable Architecture
In addition to human-friendly comparison tables, the dataset is engineered for machine consumption:
* **Downloadable CSV & JSON:** Openly accessible at `https://aitoolgems.tech/prices/` under an open data license for researchers and journalists.
* **LLM Grounding (`llms.txt`):** The repository provides machine-readable `/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt` feeds, allowing AI search engines (Google AI Overviews, Perplexity) and custom agents to ground their responses in verified local pricing data.
