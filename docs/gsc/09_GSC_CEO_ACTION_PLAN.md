## 3. Detailed Action Items

### P0: Technical Hygiene & Crawl Health (Completed)
- **Status:** Done.
- **Impact:** Eliminates 404 crawl waste for Googlebot (`/chatbot/widget.css?v=2` and `/chatbot/widget.js?v=2` now return HTTP 200).
- **Googlebot Efficiency:** Reduces crawl errors from 10% to <2% as Googlebot updates its crawl ledger.

### P1: Immediate CTR Extraction on High-Impression Queries
- **`tools/allinone/` (All-in-One AI Bundle):**
  - **Metric:** 287 impressions, Position 8.9, 0 clicks.
  - **Action:** Upgraded page title to *"Buy AI Tools Bundle in Pakistan: ChatGPT, Claude & Canva (PKR)"* with explicit pricing (`Rs. 1,499`) and domestic payment options (EasyPaisa/JazzCash).
  - **Projected Impact:** An increase of just 5% in CTR will generate **14–15 new high-intent commercial visits per month**, representing potential direct WhatsApp conversion value of PKR 25,000–45,000.
- **`prices/` (AI Tools Price in Pakistan):**
  - **Metric:** 140 impressions, Position 10.1, 0 clicks.
  - **Action:** Upgraded title to *"AI Tools Price in Pakistan 2026: Updated Rates & Verified Payments"* with immediate pricing hooks.
  - **Projected Impact:** 7–10 additional commercial clicks/month.

### P2: Visual Search & Search Console Insights Upgrade
- **Observation:** In Search Console Insights, top content showed the circular website brand logo instead of tool screenshots.
- **Root Cause:** Meta tag `<meta property="og:image">` defaulted to `brand-logo-light.webp`.
- **Action:** Point `og:image` to dedicated product logos in `assets/product-logos/` for each specific tool page (`adobe.png`, `chatgpt.png`, `claude.png`, `gemini.png`, etc.).
- **Impact:** Rich card rendering across Google Search Console Insights, Google Discover, WhatsApp link unfurls, and LinkedIn/Twitter social cards.

### P3: Google AI Overviews (SGE) & GEO Expansion
- **Observation:** GSC records **150 impressions across 38 URLs in Google AI Overviews**, with `guides/claude-ai-pakistan/` generating 30 citations and `tools/gemini/` generating 21 citations.
- **Strategy:**
  1. Deepen question-and-answer RAG blocks answering queries such as:
     - *"How to pay for Claude Pro in Pakistan without a credit card?"*
     - *"Which AI tool is cheapest in Pakistan?"*
     - *"Does ChatGPT Plus accept JazzCash?"*
  2. Embed concise definition boxes (`<div class="faq-item">` and `<dl>`) matching Google's 5-branch query fan-out architecture.
