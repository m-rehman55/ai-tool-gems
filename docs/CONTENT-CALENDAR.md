## Article publishing gate

- One primary question and direct answer
- Original angle or first-party data
- At least two official/primary sources when factual vendor claims are made
- Visible authoring organization, publication date and modified date
- Relevant Article, WebPage and Breadcrumb schema
- Two or more product links and two or more contextual guide links
- Unique title/meta, one H1, descriptive question headings
- Mobile and link validation before publication
- Sitemap and `llms.txt` update
