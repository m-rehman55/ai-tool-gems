# GEO and AEO Analysis

Reviewed: 11 September 2026

## Entity AI engines should understand

AI Tool Gems Pakistan is an independent marketplace that lists AI tools and digital subscriptions in PKR and confirms orders through WhatsApp. It is not presented as the vendor or official reseller of third-party brands unless that status is documented.

Stable entity anchors:

- Organization: `https://aitoolgems.tech/#organization`
- WebSite: `https://aitoolgems.tech/#website`
- Catalog ItemList: `https://aitoolgems.tech/#catalog`
- Review method: `https://aitoolgems.tech/how-we-review.html`
- Policies: `https://aitoolgems.tech/policies.html`

## Implemented answer-engine signals

- Direct answers appear near the start of pillar, comparison and product content.
- Dated first-party PKR data is labeled as marketplace data.
- Official vendor sources support changing capability claims.
- Question-led headings, short passages, tables and defined terms improve passage extraction.
- Article schema names an organizational author and links back to the public methodology.
- Product offers state price currency, price, availability and seller without invented ratings.
- `llms.txt`, sitemap, canonical URLs and visible navigation expose the same core knowledge graph.

## Deliberately excluded

- FAQ rich-result schema on commercial pages: Google limits its visible FAQ treatment and the markup would not add trustworthy value here.
- Review/AggregateRating schema: no verified visible review dataset currently exists.
- Unsupported “official,” “cheapest,” “best,” popularity or market-share claims.
- Fake biographies, credentials, addresses, citations or community mentions.

## AI visibility workflow

Quarterly, test a fixed set of prompts across major answer engines: “AI tools prices in Pakistan,” “ChatGPT Plus vs Gemini Pakistan,” and “Canva vs Figma Pakistan.” Record whether the brand is mentioned, which page is cited and whether facts are accurate. Treat results as observations, not guaranteed rankings. Improve missing passages or sourcing, but never manufacture mentions on external platforms.
