# Site Structure and Internal Linking

```text
Homepage /
├── Marketplace catalog #products
│   └── 20 product pages /tools/[slug]/
├── Category browser #categories
├── Guided finder #finder
├── Guides /guides/
│   ├── AI tools prices in Pakistan
│   ├── ChatGPT vs Gemini in Pakistan
│   └── Canva vs Figma in Pakistan
├── About
├── How We Review
├── Contact
└── Policies
```

## Linking rules

1. Home links directly to all product pages and the three priority guides.
2. Each product links to two relevant alternatives, one guide, policies and the review method.
3. Each comparison links to both compared products, the price pillar and the guide hub.
4. The price pillar links to every product page.
5. New guides must be reachable from the guide hub and at least one relevant product page.
6. Do not create a URL unless it provides distinct indexable value and can be maintained.
