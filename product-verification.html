Product Verification — AIToolGems

Our Product Verification Process

We verify all products before publishing. Our verification process includes:

  • Product identity verification: We verify product identity against official vendor sources.
  • Product feature verification: We verify product features against official documentation.
  • Product availability verification: We confirm availability via WhatsApp before publishing.
  • Product pricing verification: We verify pricing against official vendor sources and marketplace listings.
  • Product delivery verification: We verify delivery times and methods.
  • Product warranty verification: We confirm warranty and replacement policies.

Verification Process

  1. Collect product information from official vendor sources.
  2. Verify product identity against official vendor sources.
  3. Verify product features against official documentation.
  4. Confirm availability via WhatsApp.
  5. Verify pricing against official vendor sources.
  6. Verify delivery times and methods.
  7. Confirm warranty and replacement policies.
  8. Publish verified product information with source links.
  9. Re-verify before each order.

Sources

  • Official vendor websites
  • Marketplace listings
  • WhatsApp verification
