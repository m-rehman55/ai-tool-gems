Our Verification Process

Every product we sell goes through a rigorous multi-step verification process. This ensures that what you receive matches exactly what you paid for — in terms of features, access type, duration, and warranty coverage.

Our verification team tests each product type differently. For subscription-based tools like ChatGPT Plus or Canva Pro, we verify account access, feature availability, and billing status. For license keys like Windows 11 Pro, we check activation validity and duration.

Step-by-Step Verification

  1. Order Received — We receive your order details and payment confirmation via WhatsApp
  2. Product Preparation — We prepare the product (account, invitation, or license key) according to your order
  3. Testing — We verify the product works as described, check all features, and confirm the access type
  4. Documentation — We document the delivery details, warranty period, and activation instructions
  5. Delivery — We send you the product via WhatsApp with clear activation instructions
  6. Post-Delivery Check — We follow up to ensure everything is working correctly

Warranty Coverage

Every product comes with a replacement warranty. If the product we deliver does not match what was advertised — wrong access type, shorter duration, or features not working — we replace it free of charge within the warranty period.

Warranty periods vary by product and are clearly listed on each product page. For most subscription-based products, the warranty is 7 days. For license keys and longer-term plans, it may extend to 30 days or more.
