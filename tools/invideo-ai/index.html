About InVideo AI

InVideo AI by InVideo Inc. is a cloud-based generative video engine designed to eliminate the complexity of scriptwriting, video sourcing, voiceover recording, and editing. By entering a simple prompt (e.g., 'Create a 60-second YouTube Short about the top 5 emerging AI technologies in 2026'), InVideo AI writes the script, selects matching licensed footage from iStock and Storyblocks, generates realistic AI narration, and compiles a finished video.

Key Features

  • Prompt-to-Video Pipeline: Generates full video narrative, scene transitions, footage selection, and voiceover from a single text prompt.
  • Millions of Premium Stock Media Assets: Direct integration with iStock, Shutterstock, and Storyblocks for high-resolution cinematic clips.
  • Hyper-Realistic AI Voiceovers: Natural human-sounding voices across various accents, tones, and languages with dynamic pacing.
  • Natural Language Video Editing: Type commands like 'Delete the second scene' or 'Make the voiceover more energetic' to edit effortlessly.
  • Multi-Platform Aspect Ratios: Instantly format videos for 16:9 YouTube, 9:16 Shorts/TikTok/Reels, or 1:1 LinkedIn/Facebook posts.

Primary Use Cases

  • Faceless YouTube Channels: Automate daily video creation in niches like history, finance, motivation, and technology.
  • Real Estate Video Marketing: Generate compelling property showcase videos with cinematic music and professional voiceovers.
  • Social Media Ad Creatives: Quickly produce multiple video advertising variations for Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok ads.

Frequently asked questions

Can I monetize InVideo AI videos on YouTube in Pakistan?

Yes, videos created with paid InVideo plans (Plus or Max) include commercial licenses for all footage, music, and voiceovers, making them fully eligible for YouTube partner monetization.

How does InVideo AI compare to CapCut?

CapCut is a manual timeline video editor where you cut and arrange footage yourself; InVideo AI generates the script, voiceover, stock footage, and edits automatically from a single sentence prompt.

Can InVideo AI create videos in Urdu?

Yes, InVideo AI supports scriptwriting and voiceovers in Urdu, as well as over 50 other international languages.

What payment options work for InVideo in Pakistan?

Pakistani users can pay using international international cards from SadaPay, NayaPay, or Meezan Bank.

Can I clone my own voice in InVideo AI?

Yes, the InVideo Max plan supports voice cloning, allowing you to narrate your automated videos in your own voice without speaking each script.
