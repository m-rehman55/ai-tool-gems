About Google NotebookLM

Google NotebookLM is Google's breakout research and thinking assistant built on Gemini Pro's massive context architecture. Unlike open-ended AI chatbots that hallucinate or browse random internet content, NotebookLM is strictly 'grounded' in the specific source materials you upload (PDFs, docs, research papers, YouTube videos). It features direct citation numbers linking to exact source pages, and went viral worldwide for its 'Audio Overview' feature which generates engaging, human-sounding deep-dive podcast discussions from any material.
