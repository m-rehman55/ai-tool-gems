About Google NotebookLM

Personalized AI research assistant that grounds its thinking exclusively in your uploaded source materials.

Google NotebookLM is a specialized research environment that pairs Google's frontier Gemini 1.5 Pro model with your uploaded documents, PDFs, notes, and transcripts. Unlike generic conversational chatbots that hallucinate external facts, NotebookLM grounds all responses strictly within your uploaded materials, providing direct clickable citations and generating viral 2-speaker podcast discussions (Audio Overviews).
