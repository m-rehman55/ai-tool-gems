About Ahrefs

The gold-standard SEO and backlink intelligence platform trusted by top digital marketing agencies.

Ahrefs is the world's leading backlink analysis and keyword research platform, crawling over 8 billion web pages daily. It provides verified organic search data, competitor keyword gaps, toxic backlink audits, and actionable rank tracking.
