About Ahrefs Free & Starter

Industry-standard SEO intelligence offering a robust free tier for verified website owners alongside budget-friendly entry plans.

Ahrefs is widely regarded as the gold standard in search engine optimization, crawling billions of web pages daily to maintain a live 40+ trillion link index. For individual creators, webmasters, and growing businesses in Pakistan, Ahrefs provides two practical entry points: Ahrefs Free (formerly Ahrefs Webmaster Tools), which offers permanent free SEO health monitoring for websites you verify ownership of, and the official Starter Plan ($29/month), which unlocks light competitive research across external domains without committing to high-tier agency pricing.

Primary Use Cases

Technical Site AuditsBacklink MonitoringKeyword ResearchWebmaster Analytics

Key Capabilities

  • Free Site Audit: Crawls verified websites for 170+ on-page and technical SEO issues with 5,000 monthly credits.
  • Site Explorer for Owned Domains: Monitors organic keyword rankings, referring domains, and backlink growth up to 1,000 rows.
  • Starter Plan ($29/mo): Grants 200 monthly credits across Site Explorer and Keywords Explorer for light competitor analysis.
  • Rank Tracker (Starter): Tracks up to 50 search keywords with daily or weekly updates.

Limitations & Considerations

  • Ownership Verification Required for Free Tier: Ahrefs Free is restricted to websites where you verify ownership via Google Search Console, DNS TXT record, or HTML upload.
  • Starter Tier Usage Caps: The $29/mo plan strictly limits consumption to 200 monthly credits, 1 unverified competitor project, and 250 rows per report.
  • Strict Single-User Seat Enforcement: Ahrefs enforces strict single-seat login policies; sharing credentials across multiple users triggers automated account suspensions.
