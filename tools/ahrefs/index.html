About Ahrefs (Free & Starter)

Ahrefs is globally recognized as the gold standard in search engine optimization, backlink analysis, and keyword intelligence. Crawling over 8 billion web pages daily with its proprietary crawler (AhrefsBot), Ahrefs provides the most accurate link graph and organic search data in the industry. For website owners, Ahrefs Webmaster Tools (AWT) provides enterprise-grade technical site audits and keyword tracking completely free.

Primary Use Cases & Workflows

Technical SEO Health Audits

Identify indexability issues, broken redirects, slow pages, and missing canonical tags across your site.

Competitor Keyword Gap Analysis

Find keywords your competitors rank for in Pakistan and globally that your website is missing.

Backlink Building & Outreach

Uncover link-building opportunities by analyzing who links to competitor articles in your niche.

Client SEO Reporting for Agencies

Generate authoritative domain authority and keyword ranking reports for agency clients.

Key Capabilities & Technical Features

  • World's #1 Backlink Index: Inspect any domain's backlink profile, anchor texts, referring domains, and domain rating (DR).
  • Ahrefs Webmaster Tools (100% Free): Free site audit and rank tracking for your own verified websites with 5,000 crawl credits/month.
  • Keywords Explorer with Search Volume: Discover keyword difficulty (KD), search volumes across Google/YouTube, and SERP click metrics.
  • Site Explorer & Competitor Organic Traffic: Analyze competitor websites to see which pages generate their organic traffic and revenue.
  • Content Explorer & Broken Link Checker: Discover high-performing content in any niche and identify high-authority broken link opportunities.

Limitations & Considerations

  • Expensive Commercial Tiers: Standard Lite and Standard plans ($99 to $199/mo) are costly for individual Pakistani freelancers.
  • Credit-Based Consumption System: Ahrefs uses a strict credit consumption model where running reports and filtering consumes user credits.
  • Free Tool Requires Ownership Verification: The free AWT tier only analyzes websites you verify ownership of via Google Search Console or DNS.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Ahrefs really free for website owners in Pakistan?

Yes! Ahrefs Webmaster Tools (AWT) is 100% free for websites you own. It provides deep technical site audits, backlink tracking, and keyword monitoring without needing a credit card.

How does Ahrefs compare to Semrush?

Ahrefs is widely regarded as having the superior backlink index, UI cleanliness, and technical SEO crawler, while Semrush excels in PPC competitor advertising and social media tools.

What is Domain Rating (DR) in Ahrefs?

Domain Rating (DR) is Ahrefs' proprietary metric (scaled 0 to 100) that shows the relative strength of a website's backlink profile compared to every other site in their index.

What payment cards work for Ahrefs in Pakistan?

International Visa and Mastercard credit cards from Standard Chartered, Meezan Bank, and Bank Alfalah work on Ahrefs' checkout.

Can Ahrefs help me find broken links on my website?

Yes, Ahrefs Webmaster Tools scans your website and provides a complete list of all internal and external broken 404 links so you can fix them.
