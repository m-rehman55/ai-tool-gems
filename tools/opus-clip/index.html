About OpusClip

OpusClip leverages multimodal video intelligence to analyze long-form video content, pinpointing the most engaging highlights using its proprietary 'Virality Score'. It automatically crops horizontal footage into vertical 9:16 aspect ratio, tracks the active speaker's face dynamically, generates animated dynamic subtitles with emojis, and edits filler words to maximize audience retention.
