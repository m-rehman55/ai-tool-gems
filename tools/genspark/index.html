About Genspark AI

An AI search engine that deploys specialized autonomous agents to research, synthesize, and create full web pages for you.

Genspark replaces traditional list-of-blue-links search results by deploying multiple specialized AI agents in parallel to scour the web, cross-reference sources, verify facts, and generate a comprehensive interactive Sparkpage answering your question.
