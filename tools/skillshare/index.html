Yes! Skillshare offers regional pricing for Pakistan, charging approximately $32 USD per year (around Rs. 8,900 PKR), compared to standard US pricing of $168/year.

Can I get a free trial of Skillshare in Pakistan?

Yes, new users can activate a 7-day to 30-day free trial on Skillshare using a debit card with international transactions enabled.

Can Skillshare help me get freelance design clients on Upwork?

Absolutely! Skillshare's project-based curriculum teaches you to build real logo suites, social media kits, and brand identities that make impressive portfolio samples for Upwork and Fiverr.

What payment cards work for Skillshare in Pakistan?

SadaPay, NayaPay, and Meezan Bank debit cards with international online payments enabled work reliably.

Yes, you can cancel your trial anytime in your account settings before the trial period ends to avoid being charged.
