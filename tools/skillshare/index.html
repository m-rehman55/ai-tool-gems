About Skillshare Premium

Skillshare Premium is the world's most vibrant online learning platform for creative professionals, designers, illustrators, and freelancers. Unlike formal academic lecture platforms, Skillshare focuses on practical, project-based classes typically lasting between 30 to 90 minutes. Taught by renowned creators like Aaron Draplin, Jessica Hische, and Ali Abdaal, students build tangible portfolio pieces while receiving feedback from global creative peers.

Primary Use Cases & Workflows

Graphic Design & Logo Portfolio Building

Master Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and typography to launch a successful freelancing career on Upwork/Fiverr.

Freelancing & Creative Business Skills

Learn pricing, client communication, contracts, and personal branding from top creative entrepreneurs.

Illustration & Digital Art (Procreate)

Master digital drawing and painting on iPad using Procreate with top digital artists.

Motion Graphics & 3D Animation

Learn After Effects, Blender, and Cinema 4D animation techniques through bite-sized practical tutorials.

Key Capabilities & Technical Features

  • 25,000+ Creative Classes: Unrestricted access to high-definition classes across graphic design, UI/UX, typography, 3D, and branding.
  • Project-Based Hands-On Learning: Every class centers around a real creative assignment to help you build a professional client portfolio.
  • Offline Mobile App Viewing: Download classes to your iOS or Android phone or tablet to learn anywhere without mobile data usage.
  • Active Global Creative Community: Upload your artwork and design projects to class project galleries to receive constructive instructor and peer feedback.
  • Exclusive Member Perks & Discounts: Discounts on creative software including Adobe Creative Cloud, Squarespace, and Canva Pro.

Limitations & Considerations

  • Not Geared Toward Deep Coding: Skillshare excels in creative design, video, and freelancing; it is not suited for advanced computer science.
  • No Accredited University Degrees: Certificates are completion-focused rather than university-accredited degrees.
  • Annual Upfront Billing Default: Skillshare primarily bills annually; monthly subscription options are significantly more expensive.
