Frequently asked questions

Is Coursera Plus worth it for Pakistani students?

Yes, for students pursuing Google, Meta, or IBM certificates, Coursera Plus is far cheaper than paying individually ($49/mo per specialization), allowing you to complete multiple credentials under one fee.

How can Pakistani students get Coursera courses for free?

You can audit most courses for free, or click 'Financial Aid Available' on any course page. Coursera routinely approves financial aid for Pakistani applicants who explain their financial background.

What payment methods work for Coursera in Pakistan?

Pakistani bank international cards with international online sessions enabled, as well as SadaPay and NayaPay virtual cards, work smoothly on Coursera's checkout.

Are Coursera certificates recognized by international employers?

Yes, Professional Certificates from Google, IBM, and Meta on Coursera are widely recognized by global recruiters and can be displayed on your LinkedIn profile.

Can I cancel Coursera Plus anytime?

Yes, if you choose the monthly plan, you can cancel at any time through your account settings and you will retain access until the end of your billing cycle.
