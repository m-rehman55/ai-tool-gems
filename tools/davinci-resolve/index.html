Frequently asked questions

Is the free version of DaVinci Resolve really watermark-free?

Yes! The free version of DaVinci Resolve has absolutely NO watermark, no time limit, and can be used for commercial client projects up to 4K resolution.

What is the difference between free DaVinci Resolve and Studio?

Studio adds the DaVinci Neural Engine AI tools (Magic Mask, Voice Isolation, Speed Warp, Face Refinement), support for resolutions above 4K, multi-GPU acceleration, and advanced HDR tools.

Do I have to pay a monthly subscription for DaVinci Resolve?

No! Blackmagic Design does not charge any subscription fees. The free version is permanently free, and the Studio version is a one-time purchase with free upgrades to future versions.

What PC specs do I need to run DaVinci Resolve smoothly in Pakistan?

You need a modern multi-core processor (Intel Core i5/i7 12th gen+ or AMD Ryzen 5000+), an NVIDIA dedicated GPU (GTX 1660 / RTX 3050 or higher with 6GB+ VRAM), 16GB RAM, and an SSD.

Can DaVinci Resolve replace Adobe Premiere Pro?

Yes, millions of professional video editors and studios have completely transitioned from Premiere Pro to DaVinci Resolve to avoid Adobe's monthly subscription while gaining superior color grading and audio tools.
