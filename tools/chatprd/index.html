General-purpose AI chatbots often produce generic, surface-level product documentation. ChatPRD is engineered with rigorous Silicon Valley product frameworks to challenge your assumptions, uncover missing edge cases, define measurable success metrics (OKRs/KPIs), and generate production-ready Product Requirement Documents (PRDs).
