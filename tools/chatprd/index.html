Created by veteran tech executive Claire Vo, ChatPRD is a specialized AI co-pilot designed exclusively for the product management workflow. General-purpose AI chatbots often produce generic, surface-level product documentation. ChatPRD is engineered with rigorous Silicon Valley product frameworks to challenge your assumptions, uncover missing edge cases, define measurable success metrics (OKRs/KPIs), and generate production-ready Product Requirement Documents (PRDs).

Rapid Feature PRD Drafting

Key Capabilities & Technical Features

  • Product-Specific AI Personas: Act as a tough Chief Product Officer, skeptical engineering manager, or customer advocate to stress-test your ideas.
  • Comprehensive PRD Generation: Generates complete PRDs with problem statements, user personas, technical requirements, and rollout phases.

  • Agile User Stories & Acceptance Criteria: Breaks high-level features down into engineering-ready Jira user stories with Given/When/Then criteria.
