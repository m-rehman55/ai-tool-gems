About Descript

Descript revolutionized media editing by treating audio and video as text documents. When you record or upload media, Descript generates a precise speech-to-text transcript. Deleting a word or sentence in the text automatically deletes the corresponding segment from the audio and video track. With breakthrough features like Studio Sound, AI Eye Contact correction, and Overdub voice cloning, it is the standard toolkit for podcasters and educators.

Key Features

  • Edit Video Like a Doc: Cut, reorder, and refine video simply by cutting and pasting transcribed text.

Frequently asked questions

Is Descript's Studio Sound really that good?

Yes, Studio Sound is considered the best AI audio enhancement tool in the world, capable of turning audio recorded with cheap phone earphones into pristine studio-quality audio with zero background noise.

Can Descript transcribe Urdu language audio?

Descript supports transcription in over 20 languages, but its primary optimization and highest accuracy are in English. For bilingual English/Urdu recordings, English portions transcribe with 95%+ accuracy.

How does text-based video editing work?

Descript transcribes your audio into text. If you highlight a sentence and press 'Backspace', Descript cuts that exact video and audio section smoothly without you needing to find cut points on a timeline.

What payment cards work for Descript in Pakistan?

SadaPay, NayaPay, Standard Chartered, and Meezan Bank debit cards work smoothly on Descript's Stripe payment gateway.

Can I export Descript timelines to Adobe Premiere or Final Cut?

Yes, paid Descript plans let you export XML, FCPXML, and EDL files to continue fine-tuning your project in Adobe Premiere Pro or Final Cut Pro.
