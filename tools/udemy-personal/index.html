Frequently asked questions

Is the Udemy Personal Plan available in Pakistan?

Yes, Udemy's Personal Plan is available to Pakistani accounts and can be activated via international international cards debit and credit cards.

Can I keep the courses if I cancel the Personal Plan?

No. Personal Plan is a subscription model. If you cancel, you lose access to the courses. If you want permanent lifetime access, purchase the specific course individually during a sale.

Does Udemy Personal Plan include a free trial?

Yes, Udemy typically offers a 7-day free trial for first-time subscribers to test out the courses before the first monthly charge.

Are Udemy certificates recognized by employers in Pakistan?

Udemy certificates show practical effort and technical competence. Pakistani software houses value the actual projects and code you build more than the certificate itself.

What payment cards work for Udemy in Pakistan?

SadaPay, NayaPay, Meezan Bank, and Bank Alfalah cards enabled for international payments work reliably.
