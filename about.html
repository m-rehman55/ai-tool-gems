ABOUT THE MARKETPLACE

AI tools, compared for Pakistan.

AI Tool Gems is a Pakistan-based independent marketplace that helps customers compare and order AI tools, creative software, productivity services, security tools, and digital subscriptions with prices displayed in Pakistani rupees.

What we make clear

Each listing shows the current listed price, plan duration, access type, delivery estimate, and replacement-warranty period. Availability and payment details are reconfirmed through WhatsApp before payment because third-party subscriptions and plan conditions can change.

Independent marketplace disclosure

AI Tool Gems is not affiliated with or endorsed by the third-party brands shown on this website unless a page explicitly states otherwise. Product names and logos are used only to identify the services customers are comparing. All trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.

How listings are maintained

Catalog pages are reviewed for clear pricing and plan information. Product identity and public features are checked against primary vendor sources, while marketplace price and access terms are reviewed separately. Before an order is accepted, support reconfirms availability, access conditions, estimated delivery, and applicable replacement coverage. Read the complete listing review method. Our Story

AI Tool Gems was founded to solve a real problem for Pakistani users: accessing premium AI tools and digital subscriptions at transparent, verified prices. Too many people in Pakistan face confusing pricing, unverified sellers, and hidden fees when trying to get tools like ChatGPT Plus, Canva Pro, or Figma. We created a marketplace that shows clear PKR pricing, verified access types, and honest warranty terms before you pay.

Based in Pakistan and serving customers across Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, and beyond, we understand the local payment landscape. That is why we support EasyPaisa, JazzCash, SadaPay, NayaPay, and bank transfers — methods that actually work for Pakistani buyers.

What Makes Us Different

Every product on AI Tool Gems is tested before it is listed. We verify the account works, check the duration, confirm the access type (Private, Shared, Invitation, or License Key), and document the warranty period. We do not list products we cannot deliver.

Our pricing is transparent. We show both the official reference price and our marketplace price in PKR, so you know exactly what you are saving. No hidden charges, no surprise renewals — just clear pricing and fast WhatsApp delivery.

Our Commitment to Trust

Trust is the foundation of our business. Every order is processed with a replacement warranty, and our WhatsApp support team is available to help with activation, troubleshooting, or refunds. We are an independent marketplace and are not affiliated with the brands we sell — we simply make them accessible and affordable for Pakistani users.
